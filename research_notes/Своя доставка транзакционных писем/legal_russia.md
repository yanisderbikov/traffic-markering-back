# Legal rules for transactional email without an unsubscribe link and for own mail delivery (Russia first), as of 6 November 2026

Status of these notes: research notes, not legal advice. Everything marked "primary" was read on consultant.ru through a fetch tool that returns paraphrases, and Consultant pages warn they may display an edition that includes amendments not yet in force. publication.pravo.gov.ru was not opened directly. "Secondary" means law-firm, vendor or press material. Vendor material is often written to sell compliance services, so treat its conclusions with caution.

Project fact checked in the repo (read-only): login in this backend is an emailed one-time code. `AuthController` exposes `/api/auth/register`, `/request-code`, `/verify`; `AuthServiceImpl` uses the normalised email as the username and sends a 10-minute `LoginCode` through `EmailService`. No phone, ЕСИА or Russian ID-provider login appeared in the auth code. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube OAuth providers live in the `service/social` package and look like account linking rather than login (not traced in full). This makes the authorisation section below directly relevant.

## 1. 38-ФЗ art. 18: when email is advertising, and is an unsubscribe link ever legally required?

### Takeaway
No provision found requires an unsubscribe link in purely service email, and art. 18 does not require a link as such even for advertising: it requires prior consent, provable by the sender, and an immediate stop on request. The real risk is requalification: promotional content can turn a "service" email into advertising, and FAS looks at content and promotion rather than at whether a list of addressees exists.

### Cited findings
- Advertising is information distributed by any means, addressed to an indefinite circle of persons and aimed at drawing attention to, or promoting, an object of advertising. A рекламораспространитель is whoever distributes it — [38-ФЗ art. 3, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_58968/4f41fe599ce341751e4e34dc50a4b676674c1416/).
- Art. 18(1): advertising over telecom networks needs the prior consent of the subscriber/addressee; it is deemed sent without consent unless the distributor proves otherwise; the distributor must stop immediately on the addressee's demand — [38-ФЗ art. 18, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_58968/f892dec1383709792452f18d36e7043306e2be0a/).
- Art. 18 contains no unsubscribe-link requirement, and it does not name email: the list of channels is open-ended ("in particular" telephone, fax, mobile) — [38-ФЗ art. 18, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_58968/f892dec1383709792452f18d36e7043306e2be0a/).
- Email is nonetheless treated as covered: FAS letter of 25.09.2019 № АК/83509/19 is reported as requiring prior explicit consent for email and messenger mailings — [Pravovest, secondary, via search result](https://pravovest-audit.ru/nashi-statii-nalogi-i-buhuchet/osobennosti-razmeshcheniya-internet-reklamy-2025-i-otvetstvennost-za-narusheniya-2/). The Media Rights Center also treats email as advertising over telecom networks under art. 18 — [MMDC, April 2024, secondary](https://mmdc.ru/blog/2024/04/09/reklama-i-rassylki-nuzhno-li-markirovat-i-soblyudat-trebovaniya-zakona/).
- A subscription to a reference or news mailing does not replace advertising consent, and putting the recipient's name in the email does not by itself make the audience "defined": what matters is who can use the offer — [MMDC, secondary](https://mmdc.ru/blog/2024/04/09/reklama-i-rassylki-nuzhno-li-markirovat-i-soblyudat-trebovaniya-zakona/).
- FAS guidance reported in secondary sources: Guidance of 14.11.2023 № 821/23 («Понятие рекламы») treats non-personalised promotional information as advertising even when sent to a specific address list, and practice is said to give priority to the "drawing attention to the object" feature over the "indefinite circle" feature — [law.ru, secondary, via search result](https://www.law.ru/article/22036-zakon-o-reklame). Letter of 27.06.2016 № АЦ/4624 defines an indefinite circle as persons who cannot be determined in advance as recipients — [Garant news, secondary, via search result](https://www.garant.ru/news/1779556/). FAS is reported to recommend assessing the audience from the distribution method and content, so a message to existing clients can still be advertising — [Rambler on the FAS explanation, secondary, via search result](https://news.rambler.ru/other/40186239-fas-rf-rasskazala-chto-schitat-reklamoy/).
- Vendor reviews agree on the general rule that order, delivery, payment and password messages are not advertising and need no advertising consent, and that a promotional block in them creates advertising risk — [Letteros, vendor](https://letteros.com/blog/marketing/tranzakczionnye-pisma-chem-otlichayutsya-ot-triggernyh-i-pochemu-na-nih-ne-dejstvuet-zakon-o-reklame/); [Chat2Desk, vendor](https://chat2desk.com/blog/stati/servisnyie-i-reklamnyie-rassyilki-v-chyom-raznicza-i-kak-izbezhat-shtrafov).
- Marking (ERID): art. 18.1 covers advertising on the internet, requires the "реклама" label and an advertising identifier, and does not mention email or mailings — [38-ФЗ art. 18.1, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_58968/2c4537e4796f6ff8b2736ed1b0d4fef08e14458e/). MMDC concludes email is outside the marking rules because it falls under art. 18 — [MMDC, secondary](https://mmdc.ru/blog/2024/04/09/reklama-i-rassylki-nuzhno-li-markirovat-i-soblyudat-trebovaniya-zakona/). 2026 marketing guides repeat the exclusion and attribute it to the Yandex ОРД help and FAS, without a document; one distinguishes mailing to your own base from mailing to someone else's — [Callibri, vendor, via search result](https://callibri.ru/blog/markirovka-reklamy-novye-pravila-izmeneniya); [AdSkill, vendor, via search result](https://adskill.com/ru/blog/ord-reklama/).
- Fines conflict by source and need checking against the КоАП. The text shown as the 01.10.2026 edition has general art. 14.3(1) at 100–500k RUB for legal entities, 14.3(5) (requirements for advertising over telecom networks) at 300k–1M RUB, and 14.3(17) (internet advertising without identifier) at 200–500k RUB — [pravo.ppt.ru, secondary reproduction](https://pravo.ppt.ru/kodeks/koap/st-14.3). Another source places the same 300k–1M RUB under "ч. 4.1", per violation, and describes it for calls, SMS and messenger mailings without mentioning email — [Vitvet, law firm](https://vitvet.com/articles/koap/sostavy/shtraf-spam-zvonki-sms-14-3-ch4-1/), and a 2026 VTB SMS case of 400k RUB is reported under "ч. 4.1" — [Vesti Podmoskovya, via search result](https://vmo24.ru/news/fas_povtorno_oshtrafovala_vtb_na_400_tysyach_rubley_za_nezakonnuyu_smsreklamu). MMDC (2024) cites 14.3(1) at 100–500k RUB for mailings — [MMDC](https://mmdc.ru/blog/2024/04/09/reklama-i-rassylki-nuzhno-li-markirovat-i-soblyudat-trebovaniya-zakona/).
- 38-ФЗ was last amended by 283-ФЗ of 04.08.2026 per the document header; the content of that amendment was not reviewed — [38-ФЗ, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_58968/).

### Inferences
- Omitting an unsubscribe link from a purely service email is lawful under Russian advertising law: art. 18 only regulates advertising, and it has no link requirement. No advertising consent is needed either, as long as the email has no advertising.
- Working line for this product:
  - Service, no link needed: login code, registration confirmation, security alert, payment or payout status, moderation or campaign status tied to the user's own action or contract, changes to terms.
  - Advertising, needs provable consent and a working stop mechanism: discounts, "try feature X", upsell blocks, referral or partner programme promotion, recommendations of offers, re-engagement and win-back mail.
  - Grey: product-news digests, tips that lead to a paid plan, "your balance is low, top up with a bonus". Treat these as advertising.
- Do not add a promotional footer to the login-code template. One banner may be enough to shift the whole email into art. 18, and the sender bears the burden of proving consent.
- Art. 18 has no link requirement, but a link is the cheapest way to satisfy "stop immediately", so advertising mail should still have one. A service-mail unsubscribe that could suppress login codes would be a functional bug rather than a compliance gain.
- Art. 18(2)'s ban on automatic mailing is framed around automated selection or dialling of subscriber numbers (the fetched summary mentions automatic dialling and mailing). It appears aimed at phone-number channels, but its application to email was not verified.

### Gaps
- No primary FAS decision or court ruling on a service email with a promotional block was read. Garant's 2022 review lists FAS case № 055/05/18-561/2021 and letters № ИА/43482/16 and № ГД-06-59/70, but the page's encoding was broken, so holdings could not be extracted — [Garant, 24.01.2022](https://www.garant.ru/article/1523544/). Vendor blogs describe an abandoned-cart email found to be advertising, but no case number was confirmed.
- The e.law.ru article on a "new FAS methodology" could not be opened; it may be the 2023 guidance or something newer — [e.law.ru](https://e.law.ru/1067676). No 2025–2026 FAS document specifically about service email was found.
- No FAS or Роскомнадзор primary document on email and ERID was found. The exclusion rests on secondary sources, so advertising for a third party inside an email needs a lawyer's view.
- The correct КоАП part for email advertising without consent (14.3(1) vs 14.3(4.1) vs 14.3(5)) is unresolved.

## 2. 152-ФЗ: email as personal data, localisation, processor agreements, cross-border transfer, fines, and how the delivery options differ

### Takeaway
Assume an email address plus account data is personal data. Since 1 July 2025 the operator cannot use a database abroad for collecting and recording Russian citizens' data, and the contract ground is not among the exceptions. An ESP needs a поручение under art. 6(3), and any foreign recipient triggers an art. 12 notification and, since 265-ФЗ (26.07.2026) and order № 253 (30.10.2026), a check against a new Роскомнадзор list. The Railway hosting of the user database is a bigger localisation question than the choice of mail transport.

### Cited findings
- Personal data is any information relating to a directly or indirectly identified or identifiable individual; cross-border transfer is transfer to the territory of a foreign state, to its authority or a foreign person — [152-ФЗ art. 3, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/4f41fe599ce341751e4e34dc50a4b676674c1416/).
- Art. 18(5), as amended by 23-ФЗ of 28.02.2025: when collecting personal data, including online, and in later recording, storage, updating and retrieval of Russian citizens' data, databases outside Russia may not be used; exceptions are items 2, 3, 4 and 8 of art. 6(1) — [152-ФЗ art. 18, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/cbf4e15b7c330f9372e876cdf2bc928bad7950ef/). Contract performance is item 5 of art. 6(1), so it is not among them — [152-ФЗ art. 6, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/315f051396c88f1e4f827ba3f2ae313d999a1873/). The 1 July 2025 date comes from secondary sources — [law.ru, via search result](https://www.law.ru/article/28545-zapret-na-hranenie-personalnyh-dannyh-za-granitsey-s-1-iyulya-2025-goda).
- Commercial reading of art. 18(5): first write in Russia; the Russian database stays the main one; transfer abroad is possible afterwards with a legal basis and notification; direct submission from a form to a foreign server, and logs or temporary queues abroad, are described as not allowed — [Б-152, commercial](https://b-152.ru/hranenie-personalnyh-dannyh-za-granicej). That page also says Роскомнадзор "permission" is always needed for non-list countries, which does not match the notification mechanism in art. 12 below.
- Art. 6(3): an operator may entrust processing to another person with the data subject's consent unless federal law provides otherwise, by contract; the поручение must list the data, the actions, the purposes, the confidentiality duty and protection requirements, the duty to provide evidence of compliance on request, and art. 21 notifications. Art. 6(4): the processor need not obtain consent itself. Art. 6(5): the operator answers to the data subject for the processor. Art. 6(6): with a foreign processor, operator and processor are jointly liable — [152-ФЗ art. 6, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/315f051396c88f1e4f827ba3f2ae313d999a1873/).
- Art. 12: a separate notification of intent before transfer (distinct from the art. 22 notification), naming the legal basis, purposes, data, categories, countries and the date of the recipient assessment; before filing, the operator obtains from the recipient its protection measures and termination terms, plus information on the local law if the country is not on the list; Роскомнадзор may prohibit or restrict within 10 working days; for listed countries transfer may start on notification, for others only after that period; on prohibition the operator must ensure destruction — [152-ФЗ art. 12, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/e4ebbe1780de623c7cf32a59ca82a7bb523a25dd/).
- 2026 change: 265-ФЗ of 26.07.2026 is the latest amendment to 152-ФЗ — [152-ФЗ header, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/). A BGP Litigation lawyer says the country's status now depends solely on a departmental order, no transition rules exist, new recipients or countries need a new notification, and a ban can also come on agencies' submissions regardless of the list — [Pravo.ru opinion, 16.10.2026](https://pravo.ru/opinion/266312/). ppt.ru's short review describes the criterion differently, as matching Convention 108 — [ppt.ru on 265-ФЗ](https://ppt.ru/obzory/vstupaet-v-silu/federalnyy-zakon-26-07-2026-265-fz).
- Роскомнадзор order of 16.09.2026 № 253 (Minjust № 88779, 16.10.2026) replaced order № 128 of 05.08.2022 from 30.10.2026 — [ppt.ru on order № 253](https://ppt.ru/obzory/vstupaet-v-silu/prikaz-roskomnadzora-16-09-2026-253). Ukraine is reported as removed — [ppt.ru news](https://ppt.ru/news/personal-data/ukrainu-isklyuchili-iz-perechnya-stran-s-adekvatnoj-zashchitoj-personalnyh-dannyh).
- Audit advice from a compliance vendor: map real flows, including foreign cloud, mailing and support services; a Russian database does not replace art. 12, a notice does not cancel localisation, and neither is a legal basis on its own; recheck when a provider, subprocessor or location changes — [Б-152 on Sostav, 06.10.2026](https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/293571/113022).
- Fines under КоАП 13.11 for legal entities, per one 2026 table: processing without a legal basis 150–300k RUB (repeat 300–500k); without consent 300–700k; no accessible policy 30–60k; localisation 1–6M, repeat 6–18M; no art. 22 notification 100–300k; failure to report a leak 1–3M; leak of 1,000–10,000 subjects 3–5M; over 100,000 subjects 10–15M; repeat leak 1–3% of annual revenue, 20M–500M. There is no separate item for a missing cross-border notice or a missing поручение — [Reviizor, updated 23.06.2026, secondary](https://reviizor.ru/blog/shtrafy-personalnye-dannye-2026). 420-ФЗ of 30.11.2024 took effect on 30.05.2025 and rewrote 13.11 — [ELCODE](https://www.elcode.ru/service/news/nn_subscribe_themetype_bank/c-30-maya-2025-goda-znachitelno-uvelichivaetsya-ot); the 100–300k figure is confirmed by [PCS](https://pcs.ru/blog/1105). Roskom24's 18M headline matches the repeat localisation figure — [Roskom24](https://roskom24.ru/chto_zapreshcheno_na_sayte_s_1_iyulya_2025/).
- Роскомнадзор warned in April 2025 that foreign hosting providers not meeting 406-ФЗ duties may be restricted in Russia, and warned Russian organisations of sudden termination and blocking risk — [Kod.ru, 07.04.2025](https://kod.ru/roskomnadzor-vs-hostings). Amazon Web Services and Hetzner were named in reporting on that warning; Railway did not appear in the results — [SecurityLab, via search result](https://www.securitylab.ru/news/554602.php).

### Inferences
Comparison of options (my analysis; none of the sources addresses all four directly):

| Option | Localisation (art. 18(5)) | Cross-border (art. 12) | Processor paper (art. 6(3)) | Main residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Own MTA in Russia, own or Russian-hosted server | Clean if the main user database is also in Russia | None for sending; delivery to a foreign mailbox is the open question below | Hosting provider may need a поручение if it can access data (check) | Security duties and leak fines now rest fully on the company; the Railway database problem remains if it is not moved |
| Own MTA abroad | Queue and logs abroad can be read as a foreign database; acceptable only as a secondary copy after first recording in Russia, and this is arguable | Likely yes: address and message content leave Russia and go to foreign hosting; notification needed | Foreign host likely needs a поручение, with joint liability | The worst mix: own operating burden plus all cross-border duties |
| Russian ESP on Russian infrastructure | Clean if the ESP truly stores in Russia (get this in the contract) | None, unless the ESP uses foreign subprocessors (ask) | Required; check the consent wording in the policy | Dependency on the ESP's compliance; the operator stays liable for it |
| Foreign ESP (e.g. Amazon SES) | Allowed only after first recording in Russia and only as a transfer | Required; check the country against order № 253; wait 10 working days if not listed; obtain recipient information first | Required, and joint liability applies | Getting a recipient dossier and art. 6(3) terms from a global provider may be impractical; a ban or list change can stop delivery |

- Delivery itself is probably not a transfer by the company to Google in the usual sense, since the user chose that mailbox and asked for the message. This is an argument and not a confirmed position.
- A login code plus an address is personal data in the MTA queue and logs. Short retention and masking in logs reduce the exposure under any option.
- Railway (flag only, not a hosting review): if the main user table, login-code store or logs sit on Railway outside Russia, that is an art. 18(5) problem for Russian citizens' data whichever mail transport is chosen, at 1–6M RUB, repeat 6–18M, plus an art. 12 notification for Railway as recipient. Moving mail delivery to Russia would not fix it, because the address and code would still originate from the backend abroad. The email decision and the database location should be reviewed together.

### Gaps
- No Роскомнадзор statement or court ruling on whether an email address alone is personal data was found; the conclusion rests on the art. 3 definition.
- The text of order № 253 was not read. **Whether the USA or Ireland is on the list is unconfirmed**; my recollection that the USA was not on order № 128 is unverified, so check before relying on it for SES or Railway. The ppt.ru and Pravo.ru descriptions of 265-ФЗ do not match and the law's text should be read.
- No source addresses whether SMTP delivery to a user's foreign mailbox is a cross-border transfer by the sender.
- Art. 15 (direct marketing needs prior consent) appeared only in search snippets — [legalacts](https://legalacts.ru/doc/152_FZ-o-personalnyh-dannyh/glava-3/statja-15/) — so the exact wording was not verified. The separate-consent-document rule I recall from 1 September 2025 was also not verified.
- Whether a contractual ESP can rely on a basis other than subject consent for art. 6(3) entrustment needs a lawyer. Russian ESPs' actual data locations and contract terms were not checked; that may be covered in other notes.
- Railway's actual data region was not checked.

## 3. 406-ФЗ authorisation rules and later changes: do login codes to Gmail hit them?

### Takeaway
No law found prohibits sending a code to a Gmail address, the Russian-email-only registration idea was reported dropped from Antifraud 2.0, and deputies say the fines are not aimed at users of foreign mail. However, since 7 July 2026 owners face fines of 500–700k RUB under КоАП 13.55 for not authorising Russian users by a listed method, email is not a listed method, and there is no official guidance on email OTP. A product whose only login is an emailed code is therefore in an unresolved grey zone, wherever the mailbox is.

### Cited findings
- 149-ФЗ art. 8(10), introduced by 406-ФЗ of 31.07.2023: a site, information-system or software owner who is a Russian legal entity or citizen, operating in Russia, and who provides information only to authorised users must authorise users located in Russia by one of four methods: a mobile subscriber number, ЕСИА, the Unified Biometric System, or another information system that authorises users, meets art. 16 protection requirements, and is owned by a Russian citizen without other citizenship or a Russian legal entity meeting the control test (over 50% of votes). Email is not mentioned — [149-ФЗ art. 8, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61798/78b773a28f3ad19eb234697b20ab1d48c09f748a/). Item 1 was changed by 588-ФЗ of 12.12.2023 — same page.
- 199-ФЗ of 26.06.2026 took effect on 7 July 2026 and added КоАП art. 13.55 — [Buhgalteria.ru, 30.07.2026](https://www.buhgalteria.ru/news/kakie-sposoby-avtorizatsii-na-saytakh-mozhno-ispolzovat-v-2026-godu.html); [law card with pravo.gov.ru link](https://hub.zlonov.ru/laws/%D0%A4%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B5-%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%8B/199-%D0%A4%D0%97-%D0%BE%D1%82-26.06.2026) (official publication number 0001202606260053, not opened). Signing reported by [Fontanka, 26.06.2026, via search result](https://www.fontanka.ru/2026/06/26/76502314/).
- Fines: citizens 10–20k RUB, officials 30–50k, legal entities 500–700k; repeat up to 40k, 100k and 1.4M — [Vedomosti, 09.06.2026](https://www.vedomosti.ru/society/news/2026/06/09/1204637-shtrafi-za-avtorizatsiyu). CNews gives officials 300–500k and repeat 1.4–2M for legal entities — [CNews, 10.06.2026](https://www.cnews.ru/news/top/2026-06-10_rossijskie_sajty_budut_krupno). One author notes publications disagree on whether 13.55 has a repeat-offence level at all — [Levitsky, 12.10.2026](https://levitsky-c.ru/articles/vhod-cherez-google-na-sajte-shtraf-13-55-koap).
- Political statements: committee chair Boyarsky called reports of fines for using foreign email false; deputy chair Gorelkin said the law targets site owners, not users; Avito said Google login has been banned since 2023 while login with any linked mail remains possible; site owners voiced worry about having to identify foreign addresses — [BFM, 14.06.2026](https://www.bfm.ru/news/609346). CNews says the 2023 clarification did not ban foreign addresses as a login — [CNews](https://www.cnews.ru/news/top/2026-06-10_rossijskie_sajty_budut_krupno).
- Commentators split on email:
  - Email can be a login, including Gmail, if the password is entered on the site itself, because the law regulates the authorisation service rather than the address — [Buhgalteria.ru](https://www.buhgalteria.ru/news/kakie-sposoby-avtorizatsii-na-saytakh-mozhno-ispolzovat-v-2026-godu.html); similar view, while listing foreign mail services among prohibited authorisation providers — [Klerk contributor, 02.09.2026](https://www.klerk.ru/user/2715343/707275/).
  - Email plus password is not named in the list; suggested routes are phone as identifier with email for notifications, or email login tied to a confirmed phone — [Levitsky](https://levitsky-c.ru/articles/vhod-cherez-google-na-sajte-shtraf-13-55-koap).
  - Magic links, one-time codes and password resets through a foreign mailbox are risky when control of the mailbox becomes the way in; email is better kept as a contact address — [Habr article](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1053664/). This is the only source found that addresses email OTP directly, and it cites no regulator.
- No official position was found: the sources above cite no Минцифры or Роскомнадзор explanation on email OTP. Search for one returned nothing relevant.
- Russian-email login and the second anti-fraud package: on 9 February 2026 Deputy Minister Lebedev said authorisation through Russian email addresses would first affect state institutions and that business would not suffer — [Prime, 09.02.2026](https://1prime.ru/20260209/adresa-867324055.html). Pravo.ru reports the rule on registering only with Russian email did not make it into the final version passed on 9 June 2026 — [Pravo.ru](https://pravo.ru/news/264107/). Lenta's 9 June item describes the law as "authorisation through Russian mail" but lists phone, biometrics, Gosuslugi or a Russian-owned resource — [Lenta](https://lenta.ru/news/2026/06/09/gosduma-prinyala-zakon-ob-avtorizatsii-cherez-rossiyskuyu-pochtu/).
- 41-ФЗ of 01.04.2025: from 1 June 2025, art. 15 restricts foreign messengers for informing clients; the listed subjects are state bodies and companies with majority state participation, financial organisations, telecom operators and owners of goods aggregators — [Kontur, via search result](https://kontur.ru/qa/24420). It concerns messengers rather than email.

### Inferences
- Sending a code to Gmail is not itself penalised by anything found. The exposure is the login method, so changing MTA or ESP does not solve it. Own delivery, Russian ESP and foreign ESP are all equal on this point.
- Two readings of email OTP:
  - Favourable: the company's own login system is the "other information system" in item 4 (Russian-owned, art. 16-compliant), so email is only a delivery channel for a factor checked by the company.
  - Unfavourable: the list is exhaustive; email is not named; with a foreign mailbox, a foreign provider effectively controls the factor. This reading is stronger for passwordless codes than for passwords set on the site, because mailbox access is the whole proof of identity.
- Lowest-risk direction (business decision, not a legal requirement): add a listed method as the primary login (mobile number or a Russian ID provider such as Яндекс ID, VK ID or Сбер ID, named by [Buhgalteria.ru](https://www.buhgalteria.ru/news/kakie-sposoby-avtorizatsii-na-saytakh-mozhno-ispolzovat-v-2026-godu.html)), or tie email login to a confirmed phone, and keep email for notifications and recovery. This has a real product and conversion cost, plus an SMS cost, so it should be weighed against the 500–700k RUB exposure and the absence of known enforcement.
- Art. 8(10) applies when information is provided only to authorised users and to users located in Russia. Non-Russian users are outside it by its terms.
- 41-ФЗ is unlikely to reach this product unless it counts as a goods aggregator; a quick check is worthwhile if it has marketplace features.

### Gaps
- The text of КоАП 13.55 was not read in the original, and the correct repeat-offence and official-level fines are unresolved (Vedomosti and the law card vs CNews).
- No enforcement case under 13.55 was found. No Минцифры or Роскомнадзор statement on email OTP was found, despite targeted searches. A formal request to Роскомнадзор or Минцифры could close this.
- Whether Antifraud 2.0 was signed and under what number was not confirmed. Reports also attribute bill № 1110676-8 both to it (Pravo.ru) and to the authorisation-fines law (Vedomosti), so the number needs checking. ## 4. Does running your own MTA create ОРИ, telecom-licence, СОРМ or Yarovaya obligations?

### Takeaway
Sending only the company's own notices to its own users most likely does not make it an ОРИ or a telecom operator, because the system does not carry messages of internet users and no paid telecom service is offered. This is an inference: the ОРИ definition is broad, it has no carve-out for a company's own needs, and no ruling or Роскомнадзор statement on this exact case was found. The picture changes if the product carries user-to-user messages.

### Cited findings
- ОРИ, art. 10.1(1): whoever ensures the functioning of information systems or software designed or used to receive, transmit, deliver or process electronic messages of internet users — [149-ФЗ art. 10.1, primary](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61798/9ab7abe2b9fe407f507610f7e6e14a951d575585/).
- Duties: notify Роскомнадзор (part 2); store in Russia metadata about messages and users for three years and message content for up to six months (part 3); provide information to security agencies where federal law says so (part 3.1); meet equipment requirements for investigative measures (part 4); provide decoding information to the FSB where additional encryption is used (part 4.1) — same source. An older secondary source says metadata is one year; the current text says three.
- Exemptions (part 5): state and municipal system operators, licensed telecom operators within licensed activity, and citizens for personal, family and household needs. No carve-out for a legal entity's own needs — same source. The "exclusively between users" wording appears only in part 4.2, for instant-messaging services — same source.
- Forced entry: a Government act of July 2024 lets Роскомнадзор add an ОРИ to the register after a final decision on repeat non-notification — [D-Russia, 22.07.2024](https://d-russia.ru/opredeleno-uslovie-prinuditelnogo-vkljuchenija-v-reestr-ori-postanovlenie.html). Роскомнадзор also prepared changes to the register procedure in 2025 — [CNews, 31.07.2025, via search result](https://www.cnews.ru/news/top/2025-07-31_v_rossii_obnovyat_poryadok). The law.ru item on changes from 4 October 2025 could not be opened (403).
- СОРМ commonly follows from register entry, according to a СОРМ vendor; order № 571 of 29.10.2018 sets the ОРИ requirements — [ST-SORM, vendor, via search result](https://st-sorm.ru/blog/sorm-dlya-hosting-provajderov-i-ori-trebovaniya-prikaza-571-i-poryadok-podklyucheniya/).
- Practice is described broadly: sites where users exchange messages, and even forms for communication between users and site administrators, may end up on the register — [T—J, via search result](https://t-j.ru/news/ori/); [Dept.one, via search result](https://dept.one/memo/reestr-slezhki/).
- Licence: paid provision of telecom services requires a licence under art. 29 of 126-ФЗ, hosting does not, and telematics licensing depends on providing and organising access — [Роскомнадзор letter of 20.09.2012 № 02/3489-ОГ, via search result](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_184197/). This letter is old and does not discuss email.

### Inferences
- Own MTA, company-originated mail only, no third parties: the sender is the company, the content comes from the company, and users only receive. There are no "messages of users" to transmit, so ОРИ status is unlikely, and nothing triggers Yarovaya storage or СОРМ. No telecom licence: no service is provided to anyone, and nothing is charged for transmission.
- Watch for features that change the analysis: giving users mailboxes on the company's domain; forwarding or relaying user-written messages through the MTA (e.g. reply-by-email threads between brands and creators, invitations with user-written text); in-product chat. These could look like an ОРИ regardless of who runs the MTA, so in-product chat is a product-level question that exists even with an ESP. Inbound support mail is lower risk but matches the broad "user-to-administrator" practice mentioned above.
- A plain outbound MTA has no ОРИ advantage or disadvantage against an ESP in the base case, so this topic need not drive the build-or-buy decision. Security and leak liability for personal data (section 2) is the larger legal cost of self-hosting.
- If Роскомнадзор ever asks for an ОРИ notification, respond within the deadline with a reasoned position: the D-Russia report mentions 10 working days from the demand, and forced register entry follows repeated non-performance.

### Gaps
- No court decision or Роскомнадзор explanation on a company's own transactional MTA or corporate mail was found. This section's main conclusion is therefore an inference from the statutory text.
- No current (post-2012) licensing explanation on company-run mail servers was found. Fines for ОРИ non-compliance were not researched.
- The ST-SORM statement that СОРМ follows register entry is from a vendor; art. 10.1 itself attaches the duty to ОРИ status.

## 5. Users outside Russia: GDPR/ePrivacy and CAN-SPAM (brief)

### Takeaway
Both regimes let purely transactional email go without an opt-out, and in both, promotional content can remove that status. Russian-style rules already point the same way, so one template policy (no promotion in service mail) can serve all three.

### Cited findings
- CAN-SPAM covers five transactional or relationship categories: facilitating or confirming an agreed transaction; warranty, recall, safety or security information; changes to terms or account standing and balance information; employment information; delivering agreed goods or services. They are read narrowly, and an ongoing relationship does not by itself make a message transactional — [FTC guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).
- Mixed messages are commercial if the subject line would suggest promotion, or if the transactional part does not appear mainly at the start. Transactional messages must not have misleading routing information and are otherwise exempt from most provisions — [FTC guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).
- Commercial email needs a clear opt-out working for at least 30 days, honoured within 10 business days; penalty up to $53,088 per email (January 2024 figure) — [FTC guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).
- EU: order confirmations and similar notices are commonly classed as transactional, while adding a discount code can remove that status — [Dataships, vendor](https://learn.dataships.io/whats-the-difference-between-marketing-transactional-emails); [Infobip, vendor](https://infobip.com/blog/transactional-email-gdpr). Marketing email generally needs prior consent under ePrivacy art. 13, with a soft opt-in for existing customers including an opt-out in each message — [Securiti, vendor](https://securiti.ai/blog/gdpr-email-marketing). The CJEU *Inteligo* ruling addressed the soft opt-in in a freemium setting, including mixed informative and promotional newsletters — [Freshfields](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/technology-quotient/consent-required-cjeu-issues-landmark-ruling-on-requirements-for-marketing-email-102mgiz); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/data-protection/1711932/inteligo-case-direct-marketing-free-accounts-eprivacy-and-the-gdpr).

### Inferences
- Login codes, security alerts and account-status emails fit the CAN-SPAM security and account categories and need no opt-out link. Under GDPR they would rest on contract performance or legitimate interests rather than consent.
- GDPR would apply only if the product targets people in the EU; a Russian-focused product with incidental EU users may be outside it, but this depends on actual targeting. A real EU push would also raise a transfer question for a Russian-hosted MTA or database.
- CAN-SPAM's mainly-at-the-start test is a useful template rule: put the transactional content first and keep marketing out of the subject line.

### Gaps
- No EDPB primary text was read, and national ePrivacy implementations differ. The FTC penalty may have been inflation-adjusted since January 2024; the fetched page showed that figure.
